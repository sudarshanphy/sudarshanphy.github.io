I am Sudarshan, a 5th-year physics Ph.D. candidate in the [Department of Physics and Astronomy](https://physics.utk.edu/) at the [University of Tennessee Knoxville](https://www.utk.edu/). I study how massive stars end their lives in spectacular [supernova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supernova) explosions. My research uses large-scale computer simulations to explore how these explosions unfold over time and how they shape the elements and structures we observe in the universe today.
